are not upscaled. Vanilla humanoid geometry, material IDs/stats, hand visibility,
face openings and trim support stay unchanged. Armour glow-looking pixels are
painted accents, not a block-light or custom emissive-renderer claim.

`worn-front-fit-preview.png` uses actual exported front UVs on a schematic humanoid.
It is not a concept crop, Minecraft screenshot or proof of client rendering.

change is the forty worn layers, not a claim that all 3,651 textures were reauthored.
Model facing/display values were not changed in this pass.
