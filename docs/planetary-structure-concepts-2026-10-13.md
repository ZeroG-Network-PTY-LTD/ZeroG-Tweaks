## Vent request

- Dark charcoal/black chassis and a3D approximately circular tapered cone top,
  built with coherent Minecraft facets rather than a painted circle on a cube.
- Top aperture readable from above; dark hue-shifted highlights and restrained
  planet-coloured accents, not flat black fill or gems in dirt.
- Each accepted natural cluster has3–6 DISTINCT vents, never duplicate positions
  counted as additional vents. Pick the target count once.
- Match local surface heights and preserve surrounding soil/grass; no universal
  contrasting square foundation, floating cones or broad flattening.
- Preflight protection, fluids, loaded chunks and arrival gates before writing.
  If insufficient legal sites exist, skip instead of leaving an undersized group.
- Ambient weather/vent smoke remains disabled; visual geometry is independent.
- Keep all existing vent registry IDs, matching inventory/placed models and every
  active resource-pack layer. Produce source Blockbench files and generator.
