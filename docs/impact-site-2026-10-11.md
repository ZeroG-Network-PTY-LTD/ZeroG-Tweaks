# Impact Site — barren-world Concord remnants

Two original 21×7×21 static crater templates now connect the existing Impact Site chest table to an authored world-generation route. They use craterstone, cobbled craterstone, a broken hull shell and four meteorite-fragment blocks. The low rim and cleared north approach lead to one north-facing chest with free lid space.

The existing loot table is unchanged: its Stardust pool remains guaranteed, while supplies, Star Map Fragments and trim templates retain their existing chances. No new story log, Broken Console, Wisp, Codex, inhabitant or boss was invented. The Impact Site story remains pending approval.

These ruins are separate from the timed daily comet impacts. This batch does not alter comet timing, spherical mining layers, hazards, mining requirements, recipes or gate progression.

## Placement and checking

The three registered barren wasteland biomes—Cratered Plains, Dust Badlands and Canyon Scars—are eligible. Placement uses the existing bounded dry-ground jigsaw implementation: maximum six-block terrain variation, 32-block nearby search and no guaranteed-placement bypass. Random spread spacing40/separation20 is a placement grid, not guaranteed density.

For an existing world, explore newly generated barren terrain. The operator locator is:

```mcfunction
/execute in zerog_tweaks:g2_moons run locate structure zerog_tweaks:impact_site
```

Do not confuse `/place` success with natural placement or use this locator as proof of statistical rarity. The hub and existing planetary saves are not regenerated by this update.
