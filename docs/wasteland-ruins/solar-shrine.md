# Small Solar Shrine

Original19×9×19 open Sunwarden court implementing the Galaxy5 reward route. Circular Solar Stone/Radiant Brick foundation, Gildwood approach, eight illuminated pillars, two damaged cornice variants and one accessible chest. Existing artwork and `chests/solar_shrine` loot are unchanged. Rear panels are deliberately uninscribed: no invented hymns, dialogue, Dying Star fight or ending.

Dry-land placement in Corona Flats, Sunspot Plateaus and Gildwood Oasis; initial40/20 chunk spacing/separation,6-block height tolerance,32-block bounded search and no forced unsuitable placement. These are initial generation settings, not final rarity balance.

Regenerate with `generate_solar_shrine.py --resources <code resources>`; both runtime and Design copies are produced together. Ordinary natural placement/client appearance remain separate from template, loot and native terrain tests. Existing explored terrain and the hub stay unchanged.
