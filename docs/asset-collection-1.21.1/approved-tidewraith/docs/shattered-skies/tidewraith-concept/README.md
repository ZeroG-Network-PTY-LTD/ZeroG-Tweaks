# Tidewraith — concept-first rebuild

Current visual target: `codex-clipboard-3c2846ca-efea-4721-9c8c-b0880468c70a.png`. User explicitly directed that the concept takes precedence over the previous micro cube table after rejecting its flat silhouette. This folder is the new review version; the earlier `micro/` study is retained as a rejected prototype, not the current visual target. Boss assets elsewhere are preserved.

The model now has a broader, rear-elongated 32×20×38-unit manta torso with one-unit exterior blocks and a filled interior. The mouth recess ends at the front throat rather than cutting through the cheeks or belly. A connected mouth socket, four recessed oval rim bands, swept full-thickness wings with hinge covers, inward-curling cephalic lobes and four long tentacles with five parented joints each are retained. Eight independently rigged forehead eyes sit on the actual surface and are tested for front-view visibility.

Flight uses the previous Phantom-inspired 2.417-second cadence with gentle ±6° base-wing motion. The latest repair replaces upturned tips with a downward-curled profile and adds a third wing joint: the outer wing moves ±3° with a 0.4-radian phase delay, then the terminal tip moves ±4° with a 0.8-radian delay. These are relative joint rotations, not the total world angle. Both sides mirror. Each hinge has an overlapping cover; idle and glide are slower and softer still. Numeric-keyframe playback is in `previews/fly.gif`; in Blockbench select the **fly** clip in Animate mode and press Play (opening a project alone does not start its animation). This is Phantom-inspired articulation, not a claim to reproduce vanilla animation code exactly.

Teeth are 30 small **two-sided planes**, not thick tooth cubes. A shared three-pixel-wide alpha-cutout shape gives each a broad base and narrower tip; the lower teeth flip it vertically. The old mouth clip rotated only the tooth rows while the rim remained fixed. The repaired 1.6-second **mouth_open** clip instead uses one smooth shared oval-mouth deformation (up to ×1.025 width / ×1.14 height), affecting all rim bands, throat and jaw-attached teeth together. The fixed thick collar remains attached to the head. The jaw groups have no independent stray rotation, so the tooth roots stay attached to the rim throughout opening and closing. See `previews/mouth_open.gif` or play **mouth_open** in Blockbench. This applies the plane/transparency and joint-pivot guidance in the [Minecraft Style Guide](https://blockbench.net/wiki/guides/minecraft-style-guide/) and [Overview & Tips](https://blockbench.net/wiki/guides/blockbench-overview-tips/). The complex concept silhouette is intentionally not claimed to meet every strict vanilla element-count/texel-density guideline.

The **32×32 texture limit remains**. Shared/mirrored wing UVs and an 8×8 lower-right glow region are retained. Four palettes use the same geometry and pixel layout. The fine patterns in the supplied high-resolution concept cannot be copied exactly at this pixel budget. These textures are an interpretation, not an exact image match. The back and underside are inferred from the single view.

Open `tidewraith_concept_scaled.bbmodel` for the companion-sized version, or `tidewraith_concept.bbmodel` for the large editable version. Nominal wingspan is 80 model units. Scaled projects bake ×0.1 into geometry, pivots and positional animation tracks for an 8-unit/half-block span; do not apply that scale twice in the renderer. Palette variants have the matching suffixes `_abyssal`, `_pearl` and `_storm` before `_scaled`.

Bedrock `.geo.json` and `.animation.json` exports are supplied alongside editable projects. Idle/fly/glide clips control wings, outer tips, lobes and tentacle chains; blink controls all eight eyes; mouth-open controls the shared mouth parent, with both jaw/tooth rows inheriting its motion. These assets alone do not register or implement a Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge entity. A runtime renderer and controller must select textures, run clips and render the glowmask. Emission does not automatically cast world light or create an aura.

The local Minecraft 1.21.1 `PhantomModel.java` was inspected from the NeoForm sources-and-compiled cache. Its base/tip parenting, left/right mirrored cosine rotation and 7.448451-degrees-per-tick cadence inform this rig. Vanilla uses 16° at both joints with no phase delay; the gentler three-joint delayed motion and curled geometry here are custom adaptations requested for Tidewraith. Tooth roots are snapped to the actual blue gum-band tiles, with a 0.02-unit front-face offset to avoid z-fighting. Their attachment distance is checked at every mouth keyframe, not just the rest pose.

`review.html` shows the reference beside real UV-mapped software renders and numeric-keyframe flight playback. `manifest.json` records source hashes, the generation brief, asset hashes and checks. Static validation is not visual approval or native/in-game certification. No paid generation was used. Rights to user-supplied references are not independently asserted.

Regenerate all four palettes with `python3 docs/zero-g-tweaks-bundle/generators/build_tidewraith_concept.py --all`; omit `--all` for a Standard-only visual iteration. Test with `python3 docs/zero-g-tweaks-bundle/generators/test_tidewraith_concept.py`. Generator output is confined to this folder.
