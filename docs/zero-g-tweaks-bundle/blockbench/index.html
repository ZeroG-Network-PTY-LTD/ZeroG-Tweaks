ZeroG Minecraft 1.21.1 — current designs

28 ZeroG mobs, 41 Shattered Skies looks, 38 spawn eggs, 65 drops and 52 optional aura previews. Only current generated designs are shown. Open downloaded .bbmodel files in Blockbench. Creature animations require the GeckoLib plugin.

These are software-rendered geometry previews. Animation, emissive layers and appearance still need inspection in Blockbench and Minecraft. Egg projects are baked colour previews; runtime eggs retain vanilla layers and registered tints.
