## Field notes

- Impacts are limited to one successful event per active planet per Minecraft day (24,000 ticks), with players present and suitable loaded terrain. Placement can fail; an impact is not guaranteed at a particular time or spot.
- The event searches after day-time 18,000 and reports its X/Z. Terrain is excavated; do not stand in the strike area. Arrival protection prevents impacts cutting into a protected landing footprint.
- Moon impacts can bring a Meteor Maw; that is not guaranteed, and Peaceful suppresses it. Star Map Fragments are existing lore carriers. A small Maw wreck narrative remains pending—no Broken Console mob drop is promised.
- Ocean impacts can reach the seabed. Bring appropriate equipment; admin spectator inspection is not survival safety.
