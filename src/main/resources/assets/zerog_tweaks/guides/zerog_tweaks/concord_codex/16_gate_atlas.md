Controller facing is cosmetic. Use exactly one controller and at least 1/1/2/2/4/4 Gate Energy Ports. Services sit one block above the pad, on any horizontal side in the legal service area, never in place of a structural part. Diagram service positions are examples.

Use controller **Plans → Plan T1–T6** for the standing hologram; **Build T1–T6** checks and consumes carried missing blocks. Neither guide pictures nor preview supply free materials.

Survival jumps cost 100,000 FE at T1, doubling each tier to 3,200,000 FE at T6. Admin gates are separate. Higher galaxy moons open at their galaxy tier.
