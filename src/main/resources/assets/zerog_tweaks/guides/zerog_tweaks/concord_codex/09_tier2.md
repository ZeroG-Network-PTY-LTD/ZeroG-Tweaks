# 9. Toward Tier 2

> *Moonsteel frames, a Selenite lens and this core are the next step toward the Quiet Mines.*

A Tier 2 gate is built **around** your Tier 1 gate. Keep everything and add:

* a second, bigger ring of <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:moonsteel_gate_frame" /> with an arch on the back,
* a <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:gate_lens_housing" /> on top of the arch,
* a <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:selenite_block" />, and an <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:aresite_block" /> under the centre of the pad.

Press **Preview** on the controller to see exactly where each new block goes.

## Controller upgrades

The controller has upgrade slots (one at Tier 1):

* <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:refracting_lens" />: trips cost less.
* <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:cryo_core" />: the gate charges twice as fast.
* <ItemLink id="zerog_tweaks:capacity_coil" />: 2 more passengers fit on the pad.
